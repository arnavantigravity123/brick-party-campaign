OUR PLATFORM

What We Stand For

Six key policies that will build a stronger, fairer nation for everyone.

01

Education Policy

The federal government should lower the price of four-year colleges and universities. College tuition has doubled since 2001 and student debt has reached trillions of dollars. The government should make higher education more affordable for people in poverty.

Affordable College
02

Healthcare

Increase funding for low-income individuals. Government insurance will exist for everyone, but poor citizens will receive more benefits and reduced costs.

Universal Coverage
03

Economy & Jobs

Raise the federal minimum wage by 50%. Provide extra government jobs including military, teachers, and first responders to help lift people out of poverty.

Higher Wages
04

Environmental Policy

Limit carbon emissions through mandatory carbon caps. Companies that exceed limits will pay higher taxes. Invest in ethanol fuel, electric vehicles, electric aircraft research, and nuclear fusion energy.

Clean Energy Future
05

Gun Control

Ban automatic weapons except for military use. Allow handguns and semi-automatic guns with strict background checks including mental health and criminal history screening.

Safer Communities
06

Police Body Cameras

Require all police officers to wear body cameras. This improves safety, increases evidence quality, and reduces complaints.

Accountability
