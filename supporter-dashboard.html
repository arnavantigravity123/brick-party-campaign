Party Platform

What the Brick Party stands for — our core policies and promises.

Strong School Safety

Implementing comprehensive safety programs, anti-bullying initiatives, and creating a secure environment where every student feels protected and valued in their school community.

Green Campus Initiative

Promoting environmental sustainability through recycling programs, reducing waste in cafeterias, planting campus gardens, and educating students about ecological responsibility.

Tech & Innovation

Advocating for modern technology in classrooms, coding clubs, STEM resources, and preparing students for the digital future with hands-on learning opportunities.

Mental Health Support

Expanding counseling services, establishing peer support groups, creating quiet zones, and ensuring every student has access to mental health resources when they need them.

Community Building

Fostering unity through inclusive events, cultural celebrations, volunteer opportunities, and building bridges between different student groups to create a stronger school community.
